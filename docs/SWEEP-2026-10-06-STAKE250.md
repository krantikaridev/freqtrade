## Verification (nothing here is taken from the summariser's word)

Every row was recomputed from the per-trade `--export trades` JSON inside each
`sweep/2026-10-06-stake250/<arm>.zip` — trade count, win %, summed `profit_abs`, gross
profit / gross loss, and an equity-curve drawdown built from the closed trades. All of it
matched, with one expected kind of exception:

- Drawdown from closed trades only reads slightly *higher* than freqtrade's reported
  figure (champ 1h: 16.94 vs 16.47; EmaCross 1h: 13.40 vs 13.35), because freqtrade marks
  drawdown to the intra-trade equity, not just the realised curve. Reported values are
  freqtrade's.
- Row-level `run_id`s are in `<arm>.meta.json` next to each zip; artifacts themselves are
  gitignored, so the zip/CSV pair is the receipt.

2. **1h still beats 4h on profit in every arm**, and the ordering of arms is unchanged —
   the one repeatable result from the first pass survives the stake change.

## Hyperopt — top 1 only, short budget

`SweepEmaCross` @ 1h, buy space only, `SharpeHyperOptLoss`, **20 epochs**, same window,
fee, protections and $250 all-in stake (`-i` not supported in 2026.9; single-process run).
Artifact: `hyperopt_results/strategy_SweepEmaCross_2026-10-06_16-27-28.fthypt`, best params
`strategies/SweepEmaCross.json`.

- Best epoch reported: `fast_ema=24, slow_ema=78, adx_threshold=15` → **126 trades,
  +52.48%, Sharpe 2.14**.
- Written back as defaults (`strategies/SweepEmaCrossHyp.py`) and re-run as a real
  backtest: **111 trades, +34.07%, DD 15.04%, PF 1.283, Sharpe 1.34** — *worse than the
  untuned arm it came from*.

The tuned arm is discarded. Hyperopt's headline number did not survive contact with the
backtest that was supposed to reproduce it (126 trades became 111; +52% became +34%), which
is exactly the overfit the 20-epoch buy-space search was capable of producing. The
untuned stock parameters (20/50/20) stay.

## Overfit risk, stated plainly

- Rows 6–7 are no evidence at all: 6 and 1 trades in 180 days. The RSI/BB dip-buy entry
  filter (close above the 200-EMA *and* below the lower band) almost never holds on these
  three large caps in an up-window.
- Any arm under 30 trades is marked in `SWEEP.csv` (`overfit_risk` column), regardless of
  how good it looks.
- Hyperopt output is in-sample by construction and is flagged regardless of trade count.
- The champ's ROI ladder was tuned on windows overlapping this one, so row 3 flatters it.
- One 180-day window, one regime, three pairs, no out-of-sample split. 1h arms carry ~4×
  the candles and ~4× the overfit surface of their 4h twins.
